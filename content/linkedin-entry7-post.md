Most organisations have at least one process held together with a WhatsApp group and goodwill.

I know because I was running one.

As a volunteer on a sub-committee for Protocol Directorate, my job was collating job vacancies and sharing them with members. The process worked — until announcements started getting buried. We tried a shared spreadsheet. It went stale. WhatsApp kept moving.

So I built a proper job board.

Members now get a searchable, always-current listings page. Admins post from a secure panel. Listings auto-archive after 30 days. No more scrolling back through weeks of messages to find a role that closed last Tuesday.

Entry 7 is live — the full story of the workflow it replaced, and what building for real users (instead of yourself) actually teaches you.
