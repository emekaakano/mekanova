Spend enough time in product management and you develop one obsession: closing the gap between what's possible and what's actually shipped.

That's been my career. Building products. Translating requirements into reality. Watching good ideas die in the space between strategy and execution.

When AI tools started maturing, I didn't see a new industry. I saw the same problem I'd been solving my whole career — just with dramatically better tools.

So I went hands-on.

I enrolled in Vanderbilt's Generative AI Engineering program and started shipping real AI-powered products — not prototypes, not proofs of concept, but live applications with real code, real deployment, and real lessons.

The journey is documented in real time at **mekanova.dev** — a build log of every project, every breakthrough, and every thing I'd do differently.

Product management taught me how to close the gap between vision and execution. AI is just the next frontier to close it on.

If your organisation is sitting on an AI strategy that hasn't translated into real change yet — that gap is exactly where I work.
